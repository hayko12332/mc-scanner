HaulPanel — the TMS for dispatch companies

Run your dispatch company from one panel. Free during beta.

  • Dispatch board — every load from booking to payment: broker and load number, lane with loaded and empty miles, truck and driver, rate per mile, accessorials, invoices and overdue payments.
  • Carrier statements — each carrier's fee as a percent of gross or a flat weekly amount, weekly statement PDFs, dispatcher pay.
  • Company lookup — check any carrier or broker by MC, USDOT, email or phone: operating authority, insurance and policy history, live SAFER status, fleet, state registration, UCC liens and courts.
  • New MC leads — carriers and freight brokers that just got FMCSA operating authority, with phone and email, every day. Export to Excel, Google Sheets, CSV or phone contacts.
  • MC scanner — walk any MC range and collect authorized companies with phone and email, verified live on SAFER.

Data: official FMCSA open data and SAFER. Not affiliated with FMCSA or USDOT.
