LeadHaul — new MC leads with phone & email every day

A free tool for dispatchers and brokers working with US trucking companies.

  • New MC leads — every day, the carriers that just got FMCSA operating authority, with phone and email, so you can call them first. New freight brokers too.
  • Scanner — walks MC numbers one by one (MC, MC+1, …) and collects authorized carriers or brokers with phone and email. Filters: active USDOT, with phone. Export to CSV and Excel.
  • Live SAFER check of every company it finds.
  • Lookup — check one company by MC, USDOT, email or phone: operating authority, insurance and policy history, fleet, cargo, and other companies sharing the same phone or email.
  • Public records — state business registration, UCC liens, federal court and bankruptcy links.
  • Dispatch board — a free TMS for dispatchers: loads, miles and rate per mile, trucks and drivers, carrier fees and weekly carrier statements, invoices and overdue payments.

Data: official FMCSA open data (Company Census, Licensing & Insurance) and SAFER.
