Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

HaulPanel (“we”, “us”) runs haulpanel.com: a TMS for truck dispatch companies and freight brokers, a carrier and broker lookup, and a list of new MC numbers. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: support@haulpanel.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), company name, MC / USDOT number and phone, and whether you are a dispatch service or a freight broker.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use them only to create and sign in to your account.
  • Your board: what you or your team enter or import — loads, rates, carriers, trucks and drivers, customers, invoices — and documents you upload (rate confirmations, BOLs, PODs).
  • Driver location: only when a driver connects our Telegram bot and shares their location, to show the truck on your board and on tracking links you create.
  • Connected services: if you connect an ELD (for example Samsara) or accounting software, the data needed for that feature, such as vehicle locations and hours of service, or invoices. Access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Usage: pages and features used, with a random browser ID, to understand what works and fix problems. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

To run the service for you: show your board, calculate miles and pay, make and email invoices and rate confirmations you ask for, check companies with public FMCSA data, and answer your requests through the AI assistant. We also use it to keep the service secure, to support you, and to improve HaulPanel. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

AI features

When you use the AI assistant, import files or ask it to read a document, the relevant board data and files are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the answer. They are not used to train AI models.

Google user data

HaulPanel’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account data is used only to sign you in and, if you choose to export to Google Sheets, to create the spreadsheet you asked for. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never read by people except with your permission, for security, or to comply with the law.

Who processes data for us

Service providers that run parts of HaulPanel under contract: Render (hosting), Neon (database), Cloudflare (DNS and email routing), Resend (sending email), Anthropic (AI), Telegram (the driver and notification bot), and map and routing services for miles. Your team members see the board data you share with them. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. Ask us to delete your account and we delete your board data and documents within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.

Your choices

You can see and change your data on your board at any time, export your loads, and ask us for a copy or for deletion at support@haulpanel.com. You can remove HaulPanel’s access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, connection tokens are encrypted, and each company sees only its own board.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above and tell account owners by email about important changes.
